**Market Overview**

Ultrasonic aspirators are surgical devices that fragment tissue using ultrasonic vibrations while aspirating debris and irrigating the surgical site. They are widely used in neurosurgery, spinal procedures, oncology, and minimally invasive surgery. Market growth is driven by the increasing volume of complex surgical procedures, technological advancements, expansion of healthcare infrastructure in emerging regions, and the shift toward minimally invasive surgery. Challenges include high device costs, the need for skilled operators, and regulatory delays.

**Recent Global Developments**

In 2025, Stryker received FDA clearance for its Sonopet iQ system with Connected OR Hub integration, and Integra LifeSciences obtained FDA clearance for its CUSA Clarity system extended to cardiac surgery. B. Braun invested $25 million to expand production of its CUSA Excel line. The market is also witnessing increased adoption of AI-enabled and robotic-compatible systems, along with portable ultrasonic platforms for mid-sized hospitals.
